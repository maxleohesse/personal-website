I don’t have answers

I don’t have answers. What I have are observations, questions, and curiosity.

I’ve come to think that thinking transparently is more valuable than thinking confidently. A lot of what I read online is people performing a certainty they don’t actually have. I’d like to do the opposite here — write things down while they’re still forming, and let them be wrong in public.

I don’t know yet where I’m headed. I’ve decided that’s okay, and that saying so is better than pretending to a plan I don’t have.

So this is where I’ll follow that: half-formed ideas, things I notice, questions I can’t put down. Some of it will be about AI and what people are building with it, some about running MAIN, some about neither.

If you find yourself circling the same questions, I’d like to hear from you. That’s most of the point.
